# Third-party tools and dependencies

MiniWynn's own source is MIT licensed. Its release JAR bundles no third-party libraries or resource-pack assets.

- Fabric Loader (including its Mixin integration) is a separately installed runtime dependency: https://github.com/FabricMC/fabric-loader — Apache License 2.0, with its own third-party notices.
- Fabric Loom is a build plugin: https://github.com/FabricMC/fabric-loom — MIT.
- Gradle and the checked-in wrapper are build tooling: https://gradle.org — Apache License 2.0. Original generated wrapper script headers are retained.
- JOML is supplied by Minecraft and used for matrix operations: https://github.com/JOML-CI/JOML — MIT.
- Minecraft binaries and Mojang mappings are development/runtime dependencies under Mojang's terms. They are not distributed here.

Downloaded server packs remain their respective authors' work. None of their source or assets are included.
